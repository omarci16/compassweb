The Seamless Dental Practice.

A Budapest clinic that used to lose one in three after-hours enquiries now books patients at midnight while the practice is closed. Pillar 1 + 2 + 3, built over six weeks.

Dental · Budapest · 2025

A clinic that runs itself after hours.

The brief was simple: a faster website. The real problem was operational. The practice was paying a receptionist to handle bookings only available during clinic hours — and competing clinics were capturing every enquiry that came in after 6pm. We rebuilt the site for speed and conversion, layered in a 24/7 AI booking agent that talks to patients in Hungarian or English, and added an automated SMS programme that re-engages dormant patients on their 12-month gap.

Six weeks brief to launch. The clinic now books an average of 14 new appointments per week outside of office hours — none of which existed before.
