  • Follow-ups happen when someone remembers
  • Warm leads go cold over the weekend

30–70% of inbound leads are lost at most SMBs. We route every one into a single system and follow up automatically.

02 Lost Deals

  • No visibility on whether they were opened
  • No follow-up sequence after sending
