/ The honest part

Three quiet ways small businesses leak money every week.

Problem / 01

You're paying for a website that nobody visits.

It looks fine. It loads slowly. It hasn't brought you a single qualified lead in months — and it might be actively hurting you in search.

Problem / 02

Your team handles tasks a 8,000 Ft / month system could automate.

Quote follow-ups. Invoice copying. Data entry. The work that fills the calendar but moves nothing forward.

Problem / 03

You lose leads at night because nobody's there to respond.

Friday evening forms get answered Monday morning. By then they've already booked your competitor.

/ COMPASS METHOD Active rollout
  1. 01 Understanding 2–3 days
  2. 02 Pilot rollout 2–6 weeks
  3. 03 Knowledge handover ongoing

We help you bring AI into your business.

First, we map how your business actually runs day to day: where your team loses hours to repetitive work, where leads slip through the cracks, and where AI would deliver tangible results. We always start from your processes — never from the technology.

Next, we pick one small, measurable process to automate first. Our team works alongside yours, embedded in your operations — not as outside consultants, but as a daily-available extension of your team. That way you see real results in weeks, not months.

By the end, your business runs faster, steadier, and more predictably. More of your time goes back to what actually matters — your customers, your team, the opportunities you've been too busy to pursue — and less to the work that's been quietly draining your week. The result is a measurable business advantage that compounds month after month.
