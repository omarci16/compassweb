/ The detail

What actually happens, week by week.

Week 1 — Diagnose

Free 30-minute strategy call.

You tell us what's broken. We ask the questions that surface what you might not have noticed. By the end of the call, you'll have a clear read on which pillar to start with — and we'll have written you a 2-page diagnostic, free. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you that too.

DELIVERABLE — 2-PAGE DIAGNOSTIC · NO COST · NO CALL FOLLOW-UP UNLESS YOU ASK FOR ONE

Week 2–3 — Design

Scope document and fixed quote.

We write down exactly what we'll build, when it'll ship, and what it'll cost — in plain language. For Presence and Operations projects, this is a fixed price. For Growth, it's a tiered retainer. You see the system architecture as a diagram, the page-by-page or workflow-by-workflow plan, and the timeline in days, not "weeks-ish".

DELIVERABLE — SCOPE DOC · SIGNED PROPOSAL · KICKOFF DATE

Month 1 — Build

Working software, every week.

You see a live preview link from day three. Every Friday we ship visible progress and a 5-minute Loom update. Most clients are surprised by how much happens in the first week — and slightly anxious in the third when polish work doesn't look as dramatic. That's normal. We tell you what to expect on day one.

DELIVERABLE — WEEKLY LIVE PREVIEW · FRIDAY VIDEO UPDATE · SHARED LINEAR BOARD

Launch — Hand-off

You own everything.

Code, repo, content, analytics, ad accounts, integrations. We train your team in a single 60-minute session and leave behind a written runbook for everything we built. If something breaks at 2am six months later, you (or your next developer) will know exactly what to do.

DELIVERABLE — REPO ACCESS · TRAINING SESSION · RUNBOOK · 30-DAY FREE SUPPORT

Month 3+ — Grow together

Monthly review against the numbers we agreed on.

If you're on a retainer (maintenance, Growth, Operations), we hold ourselves accountable to the specific outcomes we wrote in the scope doc. Every month: what moved, what didn't, what's next. If we're not earning our retainer, we'll tell you before you ask. No lock-in.

DELIVERABLE — MONTHLY REPORT · ROLLING 30-DAY NOTICE · KPI DASHBOARD YOU OWN
