/ What's really at stake

Every unanswered call is a customer dialing the next number.

PROBLEM / 01

A busy line is a lost customer.

If no one picks up, the caller doesn't leave a message — they simply call the next business. Trust is decided in seconds.

PROBLEM / 02

Half of all inquiries come after hours.

Evenings and weekends are when people decide whether to choose you. By morning, the warm lead has already booked elsewhere.

PROBLEM / 03

Reception can't be in two places.

While a customer is being served at the desk, the phone rings. At peak times, most of those calls simply disappear.
