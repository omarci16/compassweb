# CLAUDE.md — Compass Systems Website Build Brief

> **Source of truth.** Read this file in full before generating code, copy, or design. Every decision must trace back to a rule here. When in doubt, default to: **premium, restrained, problem-first, measurably useful.**

---

## 0. The One Sentence

> **Compass Systems helps Hungarian SMBs win more business, respond faster, and run leaner — through one connected stack of websites, growth systems, and AI-powered operations.**

If a section of the site doesn't reinforce this sentence, it shouldn't exist.

---

## 1. Company Context

- **What we are:** An AI-first digital growth partner for small and medium businesses in Hungary. We sit between a strategic consultant and an operating partner.
- **What we are NOT:** A cheap freelancer shop. A generic "we do everything" agency. An AI hype machine that talks about LLMs and agents to dentists. A SaaS product.
- **Founder context (internal only — never published):** Fresh 50/50 partnership rebuilding the brand from a legacy marketing agency. Two founders, no large client roster yet. Site must therefore lean on **process credibility, transparent pricing, and conviction** — not inflated client counts.
- **Endgame:** Land clients on Presence (websites, fixed price) → expand into Growth (retainer) → upsell Operations (project + retainer). Long-term goal is becoming the operating partner of record for SMBs, with deep MRR.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Psychology

### Two archetypes — the site must serve both simultaneously

**A. The Pragmatist** — Hungarian SMB owner (dentist, furniture shop owner, logistics manager, restaurant operator). Doesn't care about AI. Wants: a good website, more leads, less admin chaos. Wins them with: clean service pages, transparent pricing, peer case studies (their industry), fast quote process.

**B. The Forward-Thinker** — Younger SMB owner / operations lead who has heard about AI and wants to understand what it means commercially. Wins them with: ROI framing, real numbers (hours saved, response times, lead recovery rates), and the fact that we speak both languages — business and technical.

### Core psychological profile

- Skeptical of agencies (been burned before).
- Time-poor and slightly overwhelmed.
- Smart, but not tech-savvy. They run businesses.
- Want to feel in control, not sold to.
- Respond to: concrete numbers, peer examples, transparency, directness.
- Fear: wasting money, losing control, complexity, lock-in.

### The Golden Rule of Copy

> **Lead with the PROBLEM or the OUTCOME. Never lead with the technology.**

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "We use AI-powered web development." | "Your website is costing you customers." |
| "We deploy n8n agentic workflows." | "You're losing 6 hours a week to tasks a system could handle." |
| "Cutting-edge LLM-powered marketing." | "An always-on system that replies to every inbound lead in under 60 seconds." |

## 3. The Three Pillars (Service Architecture)

The whole site is built around this. Order matters: most clients enter at Pillar 1 and graduate up.

### Pillar 1 — PRESENCE *("Your digital foundation.")*

Professional websites, fast and technically clean, at fair transparent pricing. The trust-builder.

**Three tiers, public pricing:**
1. **Starter / Landing** — single-page, high-conversion. For service businesses, freelancers, new ventures.
2. **Business** — multi-page, CMS, SEO baseline, Google Business, basic CRM hookup.
3. **E-commerce / Advanced** — full store or advanced site. Payment gateways, product management, deep SEO, analytics.

**Monthly add-ons:**
- Maintenance & hosting
- Active SEO (technical health + content)
- **GEO — Generative Engine Optimization** (appearing in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE answers). This is a *differentiator* — explain plainly.
- Basic CRM & contact automation
- Számlázz.hu / Billingo invoicing hookup

### Pillar 2 — GROWTH *("Your marketing engine.")*

Monthly retainers. Lead generation, nurture, content, paid media — all AI-assisted.

**Modules:**
- Content automation system (social/blog/email, AI-generated, human-reviewed, scheduled)
- CRM setup & pipeline automation
- AI lead generation (scrape, outreach, qualify)
- Paid media management (Meta, Google — AI-optimized)
- Email & SMS nurture flows
- AI chatbot for 24/7 website lead capture

**Pricing:** monthly retainer tiers (Starter / Pro / Scale). Public "from X" with custom quote CTA.

### Pillar 3 — OPERATIONS LAYER *("Your AI back-office.")*

Highest-value, highest-margin, biggest differentiator. Custom AI systems that reduce repetitive internal work and give measurable hours back at a fraction of a human hire's cost.

**Services:**
- Agentic automations (multi-step AI workflows — "the employee that never sleeps")
- ERP integration & custom dashboards
- Internal workflow automation (email ↔ CRM ↔ accounting ↔ WhatsApp)
- AI employees / assistants (trained on client business context)
- Speed-to-lead systems (inbound form at 11pm → response in 90 seconds)
- Invoice processing automation (Számlázz.hu, Billingo integrations)
- Custom projects (priced per scope)

**ROI framing is mandatory on every Operations module.** Every service description must include: hours saved per week/month, OR error rate reduction, OR revenue recovered.

**Pricing:** project fee + monthly maintenance retainer. Show example project starting-from prices, not full price lists.

### Hungarian operational anchors (USE THESE — they're trust signals)

- Reference **Szocho tax** and the real cost of headcount in Hungary when framing Operations ROI.
- Mention **Számlázz.hu** and **Billingo** integrations explicitly.
- Mention **GDPR-compliant data handling** as a default.
- Default response time promise: **1 business day**.

## 5. Brand Voice & Copy Rules

### Tonal references (study these)
- **Linear.app** — sparse, confident, every word earns its place.
- **Stripe** — explains complex things simply; respects the reader's intelligence.
- **Apple (2012–2018)** — short sentences, sensory language about outcomes, emotional clarity.
- **2010s BMW / Mercedes** — never explains the engineering. Makes you feel the outcome.
- **Vercel** — edge, speed, trust through restraint.

### Voice rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
- Confident, not arrogant. Direct, not cold. Warm, not soft.
- Sentences under 20 words in body copy. Headlines under 15.
- Use **"you" / "your" at least 2× more often than "we" / "our"** in any given section.
- Never start a section with "We are…". Start with the client's problem or outcome.
- Every claim about results must carry a number or specificity anchor.
- Numbers and data are design elements. Make stats visually large.

### Banned words (in headlines and marketing copy)
> leverage · synergy · innovative · cutting-edge · next-generation · state-of-the-art · revolutionary · disruptive · seamless · holistic · robust · scalable (unless in a technical spec)

### AI language rule
Use AI language **only where it adds clarity to the outcome.**
- ✅ "Our system monitors your inbound leads 24/7 and responds in under 2 minutes."
- ❌ "Our LLM-powered agentic workflow leverages multi-modal retrieval."

### Hungarian copy seeds
Use these as starting points / inspiration, not literal copy:
- Tagline option: *"Iránytű a növekedéshez."* (A compass for growth.)
- Newsletter: *"Növekedési Iránytű"* (Growth Compass) — "monthly email about what's actually working in AI for Hungarian businesses. No fluff."
- Audit CTA: *"Ingyenes növekedési audit"* (Free growth audit).

## 6. Design System

### Philosophy
We are building a site that could earn an Awwwards honorable mention. Dark-dominant, with controlled light surfaces. Premium and technical without being cold. Think: a top-tier consultancy that happens to build excellent software.

**Visual references:** Linear.app · Vercel.com · Basement Studio · Resend.com · Stripe.

**Strictly avoid the "AI slop" aesthetic:** no purple→teal gradients on everything, no glowing orbs, no neon city backgrounds, no generic robot/brain iconography, no spinning particle fields.

### Color tokens

```
--color-bg:           #0A0A0A   /* near-black base (not pure black) */
--color-surface:      #111111   /* cards, section backgrounds */
--color-surface-2:    #1A1A1A   /* elevated, hover states */
--color-border:       #222222   /* subtle borders */
--color-border-light: #2E2E2E   /* visible dividers */

--color-text-primary:   #F0EDE8 /* warm off-white */
--color-text-secondary: #A09E99 /* body muted */
--color-text-tertiary:  #5C5A57 /* labels, metadata */

--color-accent:       #E8D5A3   /* warm gold / champagne — the brand signature */
--color-accent-dim:   #B8A678
--color-accent-glow:  rgba(232, 213, 163, 0.08)

--color-success:      #4ADE80
--color-error:        #F87171

--color-light-bg:     #F5F2ED   /* warm parchment — for contrast-break sections */
```

**Accent rule:** the gold (`#E8D5A3`) is the brand signature. Used SPARINGLY — primary CTAs, pillar numerals, hover underlines, the compass mark. Never decorative. It's the visual equivalent of a compass needle: always points to something important.

**Light sections:** use `#F5F2ED` sparingly as a contrast break (e.g., the "Problem" section on home, or pricing). Never the whole site.

### Typography

```
--font-sans: 'Manrope', sans-serif;     /* all headings, body, UI */
--font-mono: 'Space Mono', monospace;   /* ACCENT USE ONLY */
```

**Space Mono is for:**
- Section eyebrows (e.g., `/ 01 — PRESENCE`, `[ PILLAR 1 ]`)
- Pricing numerals
- Stats and technical specs (e.g., `3.2× avg. lead increase`)
- Footer metadata
- Small-caps card labels and badges

**Type scale (fluid, desktop-first):**
```
--text-hero:    clamp(52px, 7vw, 96px)    /* homepage H1 only */
--text-h1:      clamp(40px, 5vw, 72px)
--text-h2:      clamp(28px, 3.5vw, 48px)
--text-h3:      clamp(22px, 2.5vw, 32px)
--text-h4:      clamp(18px, 2vw, 24px)
--text-body-lg: 18px
--text-body:    16px
--text-small:   14px
--text-mono-sm: 12px
```

- Headings: line-height 1.1–1.2, letter-spacing `-0.02em` to `-0.03em`.
- Body: line-height 1.6–1.7.
- Mono labels in all-caps: letter-spacing `+0.08em` to `+0.12em`.

### Spacing system
8px base. Scale: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128, 160, 200.
Section vertical padding: **100–160px desktop / 64–80px mobile.**

### Grid
12-column, max-width 1280px, 24px gutter. Full-bleed allowed for hero, large stat bars, marquees.

### Motion
- Scroll-triggered fade + 24px upward translate, 0.6s ease-out.
- Card grids: 50ms stagger.
- Hover: border lightens, subtle background lift (0.2s).
- Buttons: faint accent glow on hover, scale 1.02.
- Stat counters: count up on scroll into view.
- Page transitions: fast fade (200ms). Speed is part of the brand.
- **No:** parallax overload, particle fields, autoplay video with sound, animations >1s.
- **Yes:** subtle SVG noise/grain overlay, custom desktop cursor (small dot, morphs on hover), micro-interactions that feel deliberate.

### UI primitives
- **Card:** `bg #111`, `1px border #222`, `radius 12px`, `padding 32px`. Hover: border `#2E2E2E`, bg `#161616`.
- **Primary button:** `bg accent`, `text #0A0A0A`, `weight 600`, `radius 8px`, `padding 14px 28px`. Hover: glow + scale 1.02.
- **Secondary button:** transparent, `1px border accent`, accent text. Hover: accent at 10% opacity fill.
- **Ghost button:** no border, secondary text, underline on hover.
- **Badge:** Space Mono, 11px, all-caps, `1px border #2E2E2E`, `padding 4px 10px`, `radius 4px`.
- **Divider:** 1px `#1E1E1E`. Use sparingly.

## 9. SEO · GEO · Performance

### On-page SEO
- Unique H1, meta title, meta description per page.
- Schema markup: `Organization`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`.
- Open Graph images auto-generated from a consistent brand template.
- Primary HU keywords to target: *AI ügynökség Budapest · webfejlesztés kkv · marketing automatizálás · lead generálás kis vállalkozás · AI automatizálás magyar*. Also target English variants — some HU founders search in EN.

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
This is both a **service we sell** and a **practice we apply to ourselves**.
- Content written so ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google SGE can summarize and cite it.
- Clear Q/A FAQ structures.
- Concise, factual paragraphs with strong topical authority.
- Structured data everywhere.
- Goal: be the most authoritative source on "AI growth agency Budapest" and related queries.

### Performance (non-negotiable)
- Core Web Vitals all green.
- LCP < 2.0s · CLS = 0 · INP minimal.
- WebP / AVIF images, lazy-loaded, sized correctly.
- Fonts preloaded, `font-display: swap`.
- No unnecessary third-party scripts.
- Target: 95+ Lighthouse on every page.

The build quality of this site IS our portfolio piece for Presence. It must be extraordinary.

## 11. Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 14+ (App Router). SSR + ISR for case studies. Strong SEO baseline.
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + CSS custom properties for design tokens.
- **Animation:** Framer Motion for scroll-driven motion. GSAP only if a specific section demands it.
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts (Manrope + Space Mono), self-hosted via `next/font` for performance.
- **CMS:** Sanity.io for case studies and portfolio. (Founders update without code.)
- **Forms:** React Hook Form + Zod validation + custom API route (or Resend for delivery).
- **Analytics:** Plausible (privacy-first, no cookie banner needed in EU).
- **Booking:** Calendly embed on Contact and Operations pages.
- **Deployment:** Vercel.
- **i18n:** `next-intl` or App Router built-in. **Build EN-first, add HU as a route prefix (`/hu`).** Hungarian SMB owners increasingly search in EN; offering both is a trust signal. Default user locale via `Accept-Language` header.

## 12. Localization (HU/EN)

- All copy must work in both languages. Write Manrope-friendly Hungarian — diacritics render cleanly, but watch headline length (HU is ~15% longer than EN).
- HU uses formal *"Ön"* form in primary marketing copy; informal *"Te"* only in the newsletter and conversational chatbot.
- Currency: EUR primary in pricing tables, with HUF equivalent shown in small Space Mono caption.
- Phone number: Hungarian (+36) format, click-to-call enabled.
- Legal pages (GDPR / Privacy / Terms / Impressum) in HU first, EN second.

## 13. Hard Rules — What this site never does

- ❌ Lead with "AI agent powered by [model]" anywhere on the marketing surface.
- ❌ Look like a prompt store, a SaaS template, or an AI tools directory.
- ❌ Use placeholder copy (no lorem ipsum) in production.
- ❌ Show 12 services on the homepage.
- ❌ Hide pricing entirely behind "contact us".
- ❌ Use generic stock photos of people in suits shaking hands.
- ❌ Use purple→teal gradients, neon city backgrounds, glowing brain icons, robot mascots.
- ❌ Use cookie banners that block content on first load.
- ❌ Animate things for longer than 1 second.
- ❌ Use more than 3 font sizes in any single section.
- ❌ Include vague testimonials without a name, company, and industry.
- ❌ Ship without a green Lighthouse score.

## 14. Closing Principle

> The compass points north. Every design and copy decision should ask: **does this guide the visitor toward trusting us and taking action?** If not, cut it.

When in conflict:
1. **Clarity** beats cleverness.
2. **Restraint** beats decoration.
3. **Specificity** beats generality.
4. **Outcome** beats mechanism.
5. **Speed** beats spectacle.

Build like a top-tier consultancy that happens to ship excellent software. That's the brand.
