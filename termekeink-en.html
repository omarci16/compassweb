02 — For meetings

Voicescribe

Every meeting, written down — accurately, automatically, instantly.

A speech-to-text system for meetings, interviews and calls. It turns any conversation into a searchable transcript, a clean summary, and assigned action items in moments.

  • Accurate transcripts, separated by speaker
  • Automatic summary and decisions after every meeting
  • Action items become tasks instantly — no more lost promises
  • GDPR-compliant, European data handling
